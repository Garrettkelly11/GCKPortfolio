Work

Two games in development, chosen because they solve different problems.

  • Badland Prophets

    A Powered by the Apocalypse western set inside a sentient dust storm, where the only habitable ground is a mile-wide calm eye drifting across the land and salvage crews work its edges for whatever the storm gave back changed.

    This one is about writing a system: promises that bind without a roll, a divination move whose failure state produces true prophecy instead of nothing, duels decided by declared stakes rather than hit points.

  • Shrynks

    A modern punk RPG about psychics hunted by a theocratic order, where growing more powerful and losing yourself are deliberately the same mechanical axis.

    This one is about testing a system: a d6 pool with a surge-conversion rule that makes the success curve jump rather than climb, run through a simulator across every difficulty and pool size to find out what the rule actually does — including one hole in it I'd never have found by reading.
