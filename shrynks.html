Shrynks

A modern punk RPG about psychics.

Premise

A psychic cataclysm cracked the world and shrynks came out of it. The cause is never confirmed, only argued over in-world, because finding out can be a campaign.

Shrynks are hunted. St. Iver is a church-city covering the old eastern seaboard, Drexlin runs the surveillance, and a liberated Oceania takes in whoever makes it there.

Thirteen Strains, from Telepathy to Firestarter to Blank, each keyed to an attribute plus a skill rather than a spell list.

The constraint

The theme is identity and control. Power growth and identity loss are the same axis: push harder, get stronger and stranger, slide toward becoming an Aberration. Anything that makes a character more powerful without costing them self is out.

The dice

Resolution

Roll a pool of d6s against a DC. A 6 is a Surge, 4–5 is a Hit, the rest is nothing. Each pair of Surges converts to a crit worth 4; an odd Surge counts 1. Score = Hits + Surge value. Two or more Surges on a success makes it a Surge Success.

The pair conversion makes the curve jump instead of climb. That's the feel I wanted and also the part most likely to break a difficulty scale, so I wrote a simulator to map every DC against every pool size.

Five dice clears DC 3 about half the time, DC 5 about one try in six, and DC 7 almost never. That sets the difficulty scale.

And a hole

At even pool sizes one score is unreachable. Six dice can hit 12 and can hit 10, but never 11. Four dice skip 7, eight dice skip 15. It only matters at the top of a pool's range where the odds are already tiny, but I'd rather decide about it than find out later.
